Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026 · App: Outbreath (com.lumenwarelab.outbreath)

Outbreath does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.

Who we are

Outbreath is a guided breathing app published on Google Play by Lumenware Lab. Contact: lumenwarelab@gmail.com

Data stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following only in its private storage on your phone:

  • your settings (technique, duration, sound, language and other options);
  • your session history (date, technique, duration and, if you use the mood check, the mood you selected before and after).

This data is never sent to us or to third parties. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

In-app purchase

Outbreath Pro is sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never receive your card details, name or email address. The app only checks with Google Play whether Outbreath Pro has been purchased on your account. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment: policies.google.com/privacy.

Voice guidance and vibration

Voice guidance uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on your phone. Vibration uses the phone's vibration motor. The app does not record audio and does not use the microphone, camera, location or contacts.

No ads, analytics or accounts

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools and no user accounts.

Children

Outbreath is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Health notice

Outbreath is a relaxation and wellbeing tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Política de privacidad

Fecha de entrada en vigor: 6 de octubre de 2026 · App: Outbreath (com.lumenwarelab.outbreath)

Outbreath no recopila, no guarda en ningún servidor, no comparte ni vende datos personales. Todo lo que haces en la app se queda en tu teléfono.

Quiénes somos

  • tus ajustes (técnica, duración, sonido, idioma y otras opciones);

Guía por voz y vibración

La guía por voz usa el motor de texto a voz ya instalado en tu teléfono. La vibración usa el motor de vibración del teléfono. La app no graba audio y no usa micrófono, cámara, ubicación ni contactos.

La app no contiene publicidad, herramientas de análisis o seguimiento ni cuentas de usuario.
